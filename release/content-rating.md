# Play content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Category: **All Other App Types** (Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other).

Every content question is **No**: violence, fear, sexuality, crude humour or language, controlled
substances (the beer-mug icon is only a symbol for a spending category; nothing shows, promotes or
sells alcohol), gambling, hate speech, users interacting or sharing content, sharing location,
digital purchases, unrestricted internet, ads.
