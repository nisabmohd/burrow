# Play Store listing

**Title (≤ 30):** Burrow: Expense Tracker

**Short description (≤ 80):** Money that tracks itself. Payments record from your bank SMS. Private, offline.

**Full description (≤ 4000):**

Pay the way you always do. Burrow records it, reminds you of bills and shows what is left.

RECORDS ITSELF
• When your bank or payment app tells you about a payment, one tap saves it with the right category.
• Not the right category? Tap Edit on the notification to open it filled in.
• Learns the category you usually pick for each payee.
• Payments that match a due bill mark it paid.
• Message rules work for any bank, region and language; teach a new format by tapping the amount.

KNOW WHAT IS LEFT
• One calm Today screen: what is left of your monthly budget, and how much you can spend a day.
• Bills, subscriptions and EMIs with reminders before they are due.
• Budgets, goals, accounts, cards, loans and deposits, with net worth.
• Insights: spending by category, busiest days and a month heatmap.

YOUR WAY
• Customise Today, the New expense form and what happens when a payment is found.
• Category icons for where money really goes: rent, recharge, fuel, EMI, insurance, gold and more.
• Notes, tags, repeating payments, receipts and lend or borrow, when you want them.
• Your currency and number format; type in exchange rates for other currencies.
• Light and dark themes, colours from your wallpaper, four fonts, English and Hindi.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission: nothing can leave your phone.
• No account, no ads, no analytics.
• Encrypted backups to a folder you choose. App lock with PIN or fingerprint.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0).
